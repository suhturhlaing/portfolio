Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive view of employee information, workforce structure, departmental distribution, payroll, promotion status, retirement status, and employee service years.

The dashboard is designed to help analyze workforce characteristics and provide a clear overview of employee demographics, organizational levels, department distribution, working shifts, payroll, and employee status.

Dataset

HR Employee Dataset

The dashboard analyzes employee-level HR information including department, employee name, age, shift, organization level, payroll, salary per day, sick leave, service years, employee status, and hiring information.

Project Brief

01

Analyze employee demographics including gender, department, service years, organization level, and working shift.

02

Analyze departmental workforce distribution and monthly payroll costs across departments.

03

Identify employees due for promotion, employees approaching retirement, salary patterns, and sick-leave trends.
