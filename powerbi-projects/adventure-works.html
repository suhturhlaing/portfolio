Project Overview

Adventure Works Cycles is a global manufacturing company. This project analyzes sales data from 2015 to 2017 to understand sales performance, products, customers, and different markets.

The project focuses on building an interactive Power BI dashboard to track key business KPIs and provide insights into sales, revenue, profit, customers, products, and country performance.
